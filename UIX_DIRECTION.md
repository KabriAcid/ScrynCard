# Philosophy 11

## Consistent geometry.

Everything follows the same visual language.

* Rounded corners: 12–16px
* Thin borders: 1px
* Section spacing: 96–160px
* Container width: 1280–1440px
* Card padding: 32–40px
* Button height: 48–56px
* Icon size: 18–22px

No random dimensions.

# Philosophy 12

## Components should feel like extensions of the Scryn Card.

This is perhaps the most important insight.

The website shouldn't merely *display* Scryn Cards.

The **website itself should feel like a Scryn Card.**

Every panel, modal, dialog, form, dashboard widget, analytics card, and confirmation screen should inherit the same DNA:

* Cream paper-like surfaces.
* Fine gold accent lines.
* Deep green headings.
* Elegant serif typography for emphasis.
* Minimal ornamentation used intentionally.
* Balanced whitespace.
* Refined shadows and borders.
* Calm, premium interactions.

A user who visits the website and later holds a physical Scryn Card should immediately recognize they belong to the same product family.

# The New Scryn Visual Direction

If I were writing the official design manifesto, it would be:

> **Scryn's interface is inspired by premium print design rather than conventional SaaS dashboards. The experience draws from luxury hospitality, executive corporate stationery, wedding invitations, museum exhibition catalogs, and high-end membership cards. Every screen should communicate trust, craftsmanship, and permanence. Rather than competing for attention through bright colors and excessive decoration, Scryn relies on refined typography, disciplined spacing, subtle ornamentation, and tactile visual cues that make the digital product feel as thoughtfully designed as the physical cards it produces.**

I think this is a stronger direction than the one we initially defined. It aligns naturally with your vision of Scryn as both a **reward platform** and a **premium business identity system**, making the physical cards and the web experience feel like one cohesive brand.
